Privacy Policy for AI Document Helper

Last updated: October 3, 2026

AI Document Helper ("we", "our", or "the app") helps users scan, read, summarize, translate, ask questions about, and create documents using AI-powered features.

Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, the app may process information such as documents, images, text, and other content that you choose to provide. If you sign in with Google, we may receive basic account information such as your name, email address, and profile information provided by Google.

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide and improve app features, including document processing, OCR, summarization, translation, document questions, AI writing, account management, and subscription access.

Document and File Content

Documents, images, and text that you submit to an AI feature (text extraction, summary, questions, translation, writing) are sent over HTTPS to our server and processed by Google's Gemini API to generate the result. The uploaded file itself is not kept on our server after the request. We do not use your documents to train AI models. Results that you save to History are stored in your account until you delete them. PDF tools (merge, split, compress, convert) and Scan & Clean run on your device. Users should avoid uploading sensitive information unless they are comfortable with the processing required to provide the requested feature.

Google Sign-In

The app may allow users to sign in using Google. Authentication is handled using Google's authentication services. We do not receive or store your Google account password.

Payments and Subscriptions

If you purchase a subscription or other paid feature, payment processing is handled by Google Play. We do not directly collect or store your full payment card information.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services necessary to provide authentication, AI processing, analytics, payments, hosting, or other app functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

AI Document Helper is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information should contact us.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained only as necessary to provide the relevant features, maintain accounts, meet legal requirements, or resolve disputes. You can delete individual saved documents at any time, and you can delete your account and saved documents from Profile settings in the app or by following the instructions on our account deletion page (delete-account.html). Records of Google Play purchases may be kept where required for billing, fraud prevention or legal reasons. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription; please cancel it in Google Play.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the developer through the support contact provided on the Google Play Store listing.
